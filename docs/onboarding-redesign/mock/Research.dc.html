MACROBALANCE TODAY

  1. Sign In / Create Account is the first screen. A static list of three features, with nothing to try.
  2. Account and sign-up before any value.
  3. 13 form steps under a thin progress bar and a “Next” button. No step replies to an answer.
  4. The Apple Health step asks for permission without a primer, and there is no reminder primer.
  5. Results screen, then “How did you hear about us?” (once per device), then the Superwall paywall.
  6. The adaptive engine, the app’s biggest differentiator, appears only as a toggle on step 10.

PROPOSED

  1. Hook (3): animated hero, then two auto-playing stories (logging, adaptive burn). Skippable.
  2. Quiz (10): goal → sex, age, height (one screen) → weight → activity → goal weight with live projection → pace with plan style under More options → obstacles (new) → reply (new) → adaptive → fine-tune (detailed mode only). Goal weight and pace are skipped for Maintain, as today.
  3. Earned permissions (2): Apple Health primer, then the new reminder primer, each with “Not now”.
  4. Payoff (3): a loader that names the real steps (with Lottie), the plan reveal, and Review answers (today’s summary, with its edit links).
  5. Ask (3): “Save your plan” (account), then “How did you hear about us?” (same spot as today: after the plan, before the paywall), then the trial paywall with a timeline.

PRINCIPLES BEHIND THE MOCK

Every answer changes something you can see
Long quizzes convert only when they feel like building, not filling in a form. Weight moves a ruler, goal weight draws a curve, pace moves a gauge and a date.
Sell the differentiator, not the category
Every competitor does photo logging. Few explain an adaptive target well. Story 03 and the reply screen make “learns your real burn” the reason to choose us.
Honest motion
The loader lists the calculation steps the app really runs. No invented user counts or ratings until there are real ones to show.
Value before every ask
Primers before iOS sheets, the plan before the account, the account before the paywall, and a reminder promise before any price.
Store page continuity
Onboarding is dark with leaf and sun accents, matching the screenshots that brought people in. The app follows the system theme after.
One question, one screen, one thumb
The main button is always in the same spot. Progress is grouped into four acts so it feels short, and every step can go back.

TEST FIRST (SUPERWALL + POSTHOG)

  1. Account before vs after the plan reveal. This is the biggest structural change.
  2. Trial length: 3 vs 7 days. Benchmarks favour longer trials, but Cal AI runs 3.
  3. With vs without the obstacles and reply screens.
  4. Paywall: timeline vs feature list, and yearly preselected vs not.
  5. Stories: auto-advance vs tap-through.

INSTRUMENT
